# SL-040 — A colour-blind-safe default series palette

Milestone: Phase 2 — A polished UI · State: open · Depends on: SL-036 · Closes: GAP-026

## Outcome

A reader with protan, deutan or tritan colour vision can tell every series of a multi-series chart apart by colour alone, in light and dark mode, and the series colours still stand out from the page background: the default `--chart-*` palette is chosen by measured ΔE under colour-vision-deficiency simulation, and a test keeps that floor true.

## Demo

1. Cold start: `pnpm build:cli && bin/biac dev`, open `http://localhost:<port>/weekly-review` at 1280 px, light mode.
2. Order mix (`ScatterChart series="region"`) and Trend lines (`LineChart` and `AreaChart`, `series="region"`) draw each region in a colour from the new palette, and the chart D6 adds draws at least 5 series. `playwright-cli eval` of `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement).getPropertyValue("--chart-1")`…`--chart-8` returns the palette the ADR names.
3. Run the measurement in the Measurements section over the computed tokens: the minimum pairwise ΔE of the first 8 series is at or above the floor the ADR sets for normal, protan, deutan and tritan vision. Every series colour meets the contrast the ADR sets against `--background`.
4. In Chrome DevTools → Rendering → "Emulate vision deficiencies", pick protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia in turn: every series of the D6 chart, and every region in the Order mix legend and on the Trend lines plot, is still a distinct colour.
5. Toggle dark mode and repeat steps 2–4 against the dark tokens.
6. In Trend lines, the `Heatmap` (no `colorScale`, so its default ramp ends on `--chart-1`) still reads from the surface to a saturated colour, in both modes.
7. Reload — the palette is the stylesheet's, so nothing is persisted and nothing changes.

## Measurements

Method fixed by `docs/research/usability-audit.md` (How this was built): minimum pairwise CIE76 ΔE in CIELAB (D65) over the first _n_ colours after Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes 2009 severity-1.0 simulation applied in linear sRGB, clamped to [0, 1]. Contrast is WCAG relative-luminance contrast against `--background` (`#ffffff` light, `#0a0a0a` dark, `src/web/src/styles.css`). Implemented in `test/lib/palette-separability.ts` (`simulateColourVision`, `toCielab`, `deltaE76`, `contrastRatio`, `minimumSeparation`); `test/lib/palette-separability.unit.test.ts` pins the audit's GAP-026 numbers (ours: deutan 1.7, protan 12.5, tritan 7.8 at n=8; Okabe–Ito with black: 17.0 / 20.7 / 16.1).

What the table says:

- Our palette's failure is one pair: blue-600 vs violet-600 collapse to ΔE 1.7 for deutans (and blue/violet is the 1st/5th series, so 5 regions are enough to hit it), plus emerald/cyan at 7.8 for tritans.
- Every reference that passes 3:1 on white fails CVD, and every CVD-safe set fails 3:1 on white. Stock Okabe–Ito drops 3 of its colours below 3:1 on white — a regression from today, where all 8 pass in both modes.
- Darkening Okabe–Ito's light hues to 3:1 costs separation: orange and yellow converge for protans (2.7) at n=8. A 6-colour light set keeps protan 13.0 / deutan 6.6 / tritan 10.2 with all colours at ≥3:1.
- In dark mode stock Okabe–Ito (without black) passes 3:1 everywhere and keeps 18.1 / 7.1 / 16.1 at n=8 — the conflict is a light-mode problem.
- **The demo page cannot show the defect today.** `playwright-cli -s=sl040` over `bin/biac dev` `/weekly-review` (2026-10-16, light and dark via the header toggle): Order mix, the LineChart and the AreaChart each draw 3 series (North, South, West), so they paint only `--chart-1..3`; ours at n=3 measures normal 92.8 / protan 39.3 / deutan 55.9 / tritan 23.5. `data/orders.parquet` has 3 regions and `data/channels.csv` 3 channels — no shipped data set has a category with ≥5 values. ## Open decisions

1. **D1 — which colours.** Settled in T2. Floors (T1): every colour ≥ 3:1 against its mode's `--background` and against `--chart-label-inside`, and the minimum pairwise ΔE of the 8 series ≥ 16 under each of normal, protan, deutan and tritan simulation, in each mode — Okabe–Ito parity (its worst case is tritan 16.1). Light `#0072b2 #d55e00 #009e73 #cc79a7 #8800ff #773311 #4433aa #bb55ff`: n=8 normal 31.9 / protan 22.7 (`#0072b2`/`#cc79a7`) / deutan 17.9 (`#009e73`/`#cc79a7`) / tritan 17.0 (`#0072b2`/`#009e73`); n=5 53.6 / 22.7 / 17.9 / 17.0; contrast on `#ffffff` 3.06 (`#cc79a7`) to 9.27. Dark `#0072b2 #e69f00 #009e73 #cc79a7 #56b4e9 #d55e00 #f0e442 #8800ff` (Okabe–Ito's 7 hues as published + violet): n=8 26.4 / 20.7 / 17.0 / 16.1; contrast on `#0a0a0a` 3.29 (`#8800ff`) to 14.97. Derivation (2026-10-16, inline `node --input-type=module -` importing `test/lib/palette-separability.ts`): seed the Okabe–Ito hues that clear 3:1 on the mode's background, drop any seed within ΔE 16 of an earlier one, fill to 8 greedily by maximin over an sRGB grid in steps of 17 restricted to C* ≥ 40 and L* in [30, 65] (light) / [40, 90] (dark), objective = worst ΔE over the 4 visions. Light keeps blue, vermillion, bluish green and reddish purple. Rejected: a hue-per-slot light set matching dark's order (`#0072b2 #776600 #005522 #990088 #0099ee #cc6655 #999900 #0000ff`, 18.1/18.4/18.3/17.1) — two olives; the unconstrained optimum (29.4 light) — near-blacks `#330044`, `#550011`.
2. **D2 — per-mode values.** Settled in T2: per mode, and the order differs (series 2 is vermillion in light, orange in dark), as streamlit reorders per mode. Dark `--chart-label-inside` moves to `#0a0a0a`: white on dark-mode orange / sky blue / yellow measures 2.25 / 2.31 / 1.32. The inside label now equals the mode's background, so the background floor gives label contrast ≥ 3:1 (today's minimum was 3.09, amber-600 under white).
3. **D3 — `--chart-1`'s other jobs.** Settled in T2: `--chart-1` = Okabe–Ito blue `#0072b2` in both modes (5.19:1 on white, 3.82:1 on `#0a0a0a`); the heatmap's default ramp stays coupled to it, `to-heatmap-option.ts` unchanged.
4. **D4 — length.** Settled in T2: keep 8 (`SERIES_TOKEN_COUNT`); beyond the list echarts cycles colours.
5. **D5 — the guard.** Settled in T1: option (a), our own module `test/lib/palette-separability.ts`, run by vitest's `unit` project (its `include` now covers `test/lib/**/*.unit.test.ts`); T2 adds the styles.css palette test on it. Options were: (a) a unit test that parses `--chart-*` and `--background` from `src/web/src/styles.css` per mode and asserts the floors; (b) an assertion in `test/e2e-browser/theme-tokens.spec.ts` over the browser-computed tokens, beside the existing probes. Either needs Machado matrices + CIE76 + WCAG contrast — ~40 lines of our own (the matrices are published constants) vs a colour library; a new dependency must pass `minimumReleaseAge`/`trustPolicy` and is only a devDependency. Recommend (a) with our own small module under `test/lib/`, unit-tested against the audit's published numbers (ours deutan 1.7, Okabe–Ito 17.0/20.7/16.1).
6. **D6 — a demo that shows ≥5 series.** Settled in T2: both. (a) `pages/weekly-review.md` Trend lines gains fence `region_weekday` (`dayname(order_date)`, ordered by `isodow`) and `<BarChart x="region" y="orders" series="weekday" title="Orders by region and weekday">` — 7 series; measured on `data/orders.csv` (the parquet's CSV twin): every weekday occurs, 40–64 orders each, Monday the most in every region. (b) `series-chart.stories.tsx` `EightSeries` / `EightSeriesDark` over `FakeDuckDb`.
