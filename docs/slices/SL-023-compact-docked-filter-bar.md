# SL-023 — A docked `<FilterBar>` that stays one row tall

Milestone: Phase 1 · State: open · Depends on: SL-012 · Closes: Known-debt row "The sticky `<FilterBar>` on `/weekly-review` … covers 258 of 720 px" (`docs/backlog.md`) — the R4 exit blocker; no `GAP-NNN` (the R3 audit is closed and deleted)

## Outcome

A reader scrolling a page keeps one compact `Filters` row docked under the header — never a band that hides a third to a half of the viewport — and opens the full set of inputs from it on demand.

## Demo

1. Cold start: `pnpm build:cli` if `dist/cli/main.js` is older than `src/cli/`, then `bin/biac dev`; open `http://localhost:5175/weekly-review` at 1280×720.
2. Before scrolling: the bar shows all five inputs (Region, Amount, Ordered, Search regions, Large orders only) exactly as today, and no `Filters` button.
3. Scroll down past the bar: it docks under the header as a single row holding a `Filters` button (`aria-expanded="false"`). The docked bar is at most 56 px tall (258 px today). The chart headings below it are not covered beyond that row.
4. While docked, watch the page: nothing jumps at the moment of docking, and scrolling slowly back and forth across the dock point never makes the bar flicker between its two faces.
5. Click `Filters`: the five inputs appear in the docked card, `aria-expanded="true"`. Pick `West` in Region — the URL gains `?region=West` and the charts re-query.
6. Press `Escape`: the bar returns to one row and focus is on `Filters`. Open it again and click on a chart: it collapses.
7. Scroll back to the top: the bar is at rest with all inputs visible, Region still `West`.
8. Resize to 375×812, reload, repeat steps 3–6: the collapsed docked bar is at most 56 px (434 px today); expanded, it never exceeds half the viewport height and scrolls inside itself.
9. `Tab` from the header through the docked, collapsed bar: focus goes to `Filters`, then to page content — never into a hidden input.
10. `/crossfilter-scale`: scroll, open `Filters`, drag the Distance slider; the charts respond and the row reads `Filters · 1`. `/nested/allocation` is too short to dock at 1280×720 (it does not scroll) or 375×812 (it scrolls 65 px): its bar stays at rest with no `Filters` button — click `Partner`, the second table narrows to one row.
11. `/weekly-review?edit=true`: in the preview pane the bar docks at the pane's top edge and collapses the same way.
12. `bin/biac build` + `bin/biac preview` (`http://localhost:4173/weekly-review`): steps 3–6 behave the same.
13. `/weekly-review` at 1280×720, bar at rest: pick `West` in Region, scroll past the bar — the docked row reads `Filters · 1` (the button's accessible name is still `Filters`, its description `1 active`). Open `Filters`, switch `Large orders only` on: `Filters · 2`. Clear Region and switch the toggle off: the row reads `Filters` with no count.
14. The console stays clean throughout.

**Count:** 1 of 4 has a docked filter bar (evidence) and it has this slice's defect; 1 of 4 (streamlit) has the remedy — collapse behind a trigger — applied to a different container. A docked bar that collapses is therefore a combination no single reference ships. It is justified by two sources of truth outside the references: WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum), level AA — "When a user interface component receives keyboard focus, the component is not entirely hidden due to author-created content", with failure F110 for a sticky header or footer hiding focused elements (https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html, fetched 2026-10-12); and the WAI-ARIA APG Disclosure pattern for the trigger (https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/, fetched 2026-10-12).

## Deviations

- Evidence's bar never collapses; ours does when docked. If the rule "a docked bar is one row; inputs are one click away" outlives the slice as a product rule, T2 records it as an ADR.
- Streamlit decides collapse from the viewport width; ours decides from the docked state alone, at every width — 258 px of 720 at 1280 px shows the problem is not a mobile-only one.
- Streamlit's popover is a dialog; ours is a disclosure, because the inputs' own popups (combobox list, date picker) are already portaled and must not nest in a focus trap.

## Out of scope

- Changing any input's own size or layout (slider width, titled rows) — the inputs are SL-010/SL-011's.
- A `<FilterBar>` prop to opt out of collapsing or to dock at the bottom — no reference attribute, no request.
- The ECharts tooltip that widens the page after a resize — its own Known-debt row.
- Remembering the opened state across reloads or pages — streamlit persists only user toggles of a global sidebar; a per-page bar has no such store.
