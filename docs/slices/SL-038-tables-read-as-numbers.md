# SL-038 — Tables read as numbers

Milestone: Phase 2 — A polished UI · State: open · Depends on: SL-036 · Closes: GAP-021, GAP-022

## Outcome

A reader scanning a `<Table>` (or a GFM table, or a sql fence's result table) sees each numeric header sit flush over its right-aligned figures, and digits line up column by column, because every figure is tabular.

## Demo

1. Cold start: `pnpm build:cli && bin/biac dev`, open `http://localhost:<port>/nested/allocation` at 1280 px.
2. The `<Table data="channel_mix">` headers `Share` and `Orders` end at the same right edge as their figures (±1 px). `Channel` stays left over left-aligned text.
3. Click `Orders` to sort (desc, then asc, then none). The sort glyph sits on the label's left in a right-aligned header, and the label's right edge never moves.
4. Open `/weekly-review`. In the order-grain `<Table data="order_detail">` and the `weekly_trend` table, the numeric headers are flush right. The GFM `Regional split` table and the three Overview `<BigValue>`s show tabular figures, and `getComputedStyle(td).fontVariantNumeric` is `tabular-nums`.
5. Repeat at 375 px and in dark mode: same alignment, same figures.

## Deviations

- `tabular-nums` on the BigValue figure and delta has no reference precedent for that widget. The reason is framework's own reason for its number inputs, the width not jittering: a cross-filter re-renders the figure in place, and proportional digits make its width change under the reader's eye. State it in a code comment at the class.

## Open decisions

1. **Sort glyph in a right-aligned header.** evidence pins the glyph in the th's right padding and shows it only while sorted. Inputs.table makes it lead the title. Ours is always visible and 14 px wide, nearly the 16 px `pr-4`. Recommendation: a right-aligned header's button justifies to the end and renders the glyph _before_ the title (Inputs.table's order). A left-aligned header keeps the glyph after. Either way the label's text edge is flush with its column's figures. Settle it in T1 by measuring the label's right edge against the cell text's right edge (target ±1 px) for both the idle and the sorted glyph.
