# Select on a chart

A chart can narrow the other widgets on its page. Give it `select="crossfilter"`, then click a mark, drag across the plot or click a legend entry. This page shows each gesture, and which charts take which.

The reader-facing how-to is `docs/pages/crossfilter/select-on-a-chart.md` (`/crossfilter/select-on-a-chart` in `bin/biac dev docs`); each example there has a story here.

## Narrow other widgets

A chart's selection narrows a widget when the widget's `filters` names the selected column, and its query returns a column of that name. A widget with no `filters`, or one that names another column, is left alone. The chart that makes the selection is never narrowed by it: it keeps every mark and dims the ones outside the selection.

## Click a mark

Click a bar to select it. The selection is the bar's `x` value, so the table, which lists that column in `filters`, shows only that region's rows. Shift-click, or Cmd-click, another bar to add it to the selection, or to take it out again; click the only selected bar to clear the selection.

## Drag across the plot

Drag across a chart to select a range. On a bar chart the range selects the bars it covers, and the table shows the regions under the drag.

On a histogram the range selects a stretch of `value`, and the table shows the amounts inside it. On a line, area or scatter chart the range selects a stretch of `x`.

## Click a legend entry

A chart with a `series` has a legend. Click an entry to switch its series off, and every widget whose `filters` names the `series` column drops that series' rows. Click it again to switch it back on.

<Canvas
  of={ScatterChartStories.SelectCrossfilterLegend}

/>

A legend selects only when `series` names a column and `y` is a single column.

## Which gestures each widget takes

| Widget        | Click a mark     | Drag a range       | Legend entry              |
| ------------- | ---------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Bar chart     | a bar            | the bars it covers | with `series` and one `y` |
| Line chart    | a point          | a stretch of `x`   | with `series` and one `y` |
| Area chart    | a point          | a stretch of `x`   | with `series` and one `y` |
| Scatter chart | a point          | a range of `x`     | with `series` and one `y` |
| Histogram     | no click gesture | a range of `value` | with `series`             |
| Heatmap       | no `select`      | no `select`        | no `select`               |

A Table, Big value and Value take no `select`; they only narrow.

## A chart's pick is not in the URL

A selection made on a chart lasts for the visit. It is not written to the URL, and reloading the page clears it. To share a narrowed page, use a filter input, which writes its value to the URL.
